Prepared for West Yorkshire Combined Authority

No wrong door, and no way to tell which door was right.

You have built three routing mechanisms in twelve months. None of them has an instrument behind it. And your own accounts say the outcomes framework is being written now.

This is a proposal to measure one cohort, for ninety days, and hand you the business support outcome measure that framework is missing.

What you have already built

Nov 2025

In-Path

GBP 1.7m, four university relationship managers acting as super-connectors between businesses and the region's institutions.

Mar 2026

Business Growth Hub

Launched on an explicit "no wrong door" promise: whoever a business reaches, they get to the right support.

Oct 2025

Higher Education Compact

Partners committed to growing spin-outs and commercialisation across the region.

Three mechanisms for routing companies to support. Nothing that measures whether the routing was right, or whether the company got better afterwards.

The clock, in your words

The framework is being drafted now.

Directorates report against milestones and output targets, and work has progressed to create an outcomes framework that will support the integrated settlement. WYCA Annual Statement of Accounts 2024/25, p.39

That is the reason for the timing. An outcomes framework written without a business support measure will be written with milestones and outputs again, because that is what is available. Once it is adopted it sets the reporting for years.

You are also already running diagnostics. Your own reporting records that 259 businesses have undertaken an initial diagnostic with a business sustainability manager. We are not introducing an idea. We are finishing one you started, and pointing it at commercial capability.

The proposal

One programme, one cohort, ninety days.

We measure a cohort of up to 50 companies at intake, route each to the category of support the diagnosis indicates, measure again before day 90, and hand you two reports: the cohort report, and an honest assessment against criteria we agree before we start.

No integration. No platform for your team to learn. No procurement of software. A branded link, invitations you send, and a report at the end.

Delivery partners keep their companies and their fees. Routing points at provision you already fund; it never rations access to it, and it must sit outside grant eligibility and subsidy control.

What you get, per company
  • A dated position on a published 0 to 100 scale
  • The stage where the problem shows up, and the root cause underneath it
  • Capability against momentum, reported separately
  • The category of support indicated
  • Movement between intake and exit

The question behind the question

Is this one person, and what happens if they are unavailable? Worth answering directly, because it is the fair thing to ask of any small supplier.

  • The measuring is not done by a person. The instrument is deterministic and hosted. Companies complete it themselves. Nobody has to be available for a company to be measured or to get its result
  • Reporting is generated, not written. The cohort report is produced from the data. A person reviews it; a person does not compose it from scratch
  • Four operators, not one. Named above, with a bench beyond them
  • The pilot is bounded. Ninety days, one cohort, fixed fee. If it does not work you have lost a quarter and GBP 9,000, and you keep the reports and the data
  • Nothing is locked in. The method is published, so nothing about it is dependent on us continuing to exist. Companies keep their own results permanently

How we will judge it

Success criteria, agreed before we start.

Every criterion below is something we control or drive with you. None of them is whether companies improved. We do not claim causation, ninety days is too short to demonstrate it, and neither side should sign up to a test neither can control.

What we are not claiming

Stated here rather than conceded later.

  • We do not claim the measurement causes improvement. We report what preceded what
  • We do not claim to predict growth or funding outcomes
  • It is not a credit or risk assessment, and must not inform eligibility, grant decisions or subsidy control
  • The data is self-reported by the company, and every report says so
  • There is no peer benchmark yet. The published threshold is roughly 100 comparable observations per segment, and we are nowhere near it. Comparability here means your own cohorts over time
  • Beauhurst can already tell you which of your companies grew. We do not claim to measure growth better. We measure what is upstream of it, per company, while you can still act
